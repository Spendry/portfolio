# Interactive Tools

Browser-based tools built through guided AI development. These are not original research or frameworks; they are working front-ends, and the point is the build quality and the clarity of the result.

---

## The Goal Study Deck

An interactive study guide for "The Goal: A Process of Ongoing Improvement" by Eliyahu M. Goldratt and Jeff Cox, the business novel behind the Theory of Constraints. A single self-contained page with four views: a five-minute synopsis, the core ideas, a 50-card flashcard deck in three filterable groups, and a detail tab. It is a study aid that summarizes and quizzes the book's ideas, not a reproduction of the text; the ideas belong to Goldratt and Cox.

- **Repo:** https://github.com/Spendry/the-goal-study-deck
- **Live:** https://spendry.github.io/the-goal-study-deck/
- **Shows:** guided AI development, interactive front-end design (tabs, a flip-card deck with filtering and keyboard navigation, mobile layout, accessibility), and the work of distilling a dense text into something someone can actually study from.
